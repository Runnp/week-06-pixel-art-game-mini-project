OVERVIEW
─────────
Tashkent, Soviet Uzbek SSR. 1953.
Ministry of Agriculture — Central Administration Building.

This is the quietest level in the game.
No enemies. No traps. No tools.
Just Rustam, grey corridors, and 8 minutes.

The building should feel LARGE and BUREAUCRATIC.
Long corridors. Identical doors. Heavy furniture.
Every NPC is busy and ignores Rustam completely
until he speaks to them — then they are confused
by his presence but not hostile.

The COLOR rule: Everything is desaturated grey-cream.
Only Rustam keeps his color. His navy jacket is the
only dark blue in the entire level.


MAP SIZE: 36 tiles wide x 28 tiles tall = 1152 x 896 pixels
Style: Interior only. No outdoor tiles in this level.

ATMOSPHERE NOTES
────────────────
The USSR level should feel like holding your breath.
Every second the clock ticks. Every NPC is one more
moment of delay. Zorin is close but unreachable until
the player finds all three items.

The corridor is long on purpose.
Rustam has to walk the full length of the building
to gather everything — there is no shortcut.

The colour desaturation is the most important visual choice.
When Rustam walks these grey halls his navy jacket
should feel almost offensive — too alive for this place.
The moment he steps back through the gate and colour
returns to the world, the player should feel it physically.

After Zorin adds the footnote, play a single soft piano note.
No fanfare. Just one note. Then silence. Then the gate.
