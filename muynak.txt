WHAT THIS PUSH BUILDS
──────────────────────
Muynak is the most visually striking level. The player
arrives and immediately sees the ships — massive rusted
hulls sitting in pure sand where water used to be.
The atmosphere is heavy, quiet, and wrong.
Everything here used to belong to the sea.

MAP SIZE: 40 tiles wide x 24 tiles tall = 1280 x 768 pixels


ZONE LAYOUT
────────────

ZONE A — Town Entrance (top 6 rows)
  Cracked asphalt road tiles (Malik drops Rustam here)
  A faded "Welcome to Muynak" sign (Soviet-era, rusted)
  Kamola's research tent (top right corner)
  One working streetlight — the only light source at night
  A plastic bag blowing across the road (animated)

ZONE B — The Graveyard (middle 12 rows, main play area)
  Pure desert sand base tile
  3 large ship wrecks positioned like this:
    Ship 01 — center-left  (largest, 96x64px)
    Ship 02 — center       (medium,  64x48px, tilted slightly)
    Ship 03 — right side   (small,   64x32px, most rusted)
  Ships cast long shadows (static darker tile beneath them)
  Salt crust patches between ships (trap placement zone)
  Chemical puddle near Ship 02 (trap)
  Wire snare in the gap between Ship 01 and Ship 02 (trap)
  Old fisherman sits beside Ship 01 on the left side

ZONE C — Deep Desert (bottom 6 rows)
  Denser salt flat — white tile dominant
  Scattered fish bones on the ground (decorative sprites)
  Buried anchor sticking out of sand (decorative)
  Exit toward Seafloor level (south edge, center)
  DustDevil spawner placed at left edge (faces right)

ATMOSPHERE NOTES
────────────────
The silence in Muynak is the point.
No music — just wind ambience and the creak of metal.
The fisherman never moves. The ships never change.
Only Rustam and the enemies are alive here.
The Kamola tent should feel like the only warm place
in the entire level — lamp glow, papers, purpose.
Everything outside it is desolation.
