ARAL SEA REVIVAL — TRAP SYSTEM
Desert environmental hazards that replace combat challenge
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
─────────────────
The Aral Sea desert is not safe. But the danger is
environmental — not military. Traps in this game feel
like the land itself warning you, not someone trying
to hurt you. They are remnants of the disaster:
chemical spills, unstable salt flats, buried debris,
old Soviet infrastructure falling apart.

Rustam can avoid all traps with careful movement.
No trap is instant death. All traps telegraph before
they activate (warning visual, color change, sound).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TRAP TYPES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 01: SALT SINKHOLE
Location: Seafloor level, open desert areas
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Thin salt crust over a hollow pit.
  Looks identical to safe ground until you step near it.

BEHAVIOR:
  Phase 1 (safe):   Slightly different tile color (subtle crack)
  Phase 2 (warning): Rustam steps within 1 tile → crust shimmers
                     Small dust puff particle
  Phase 3 (breaking): 0.8 second delay → cracks visually
  Phase 4 (fallen):  Rustam takes 15 damage, movement slowed
                     50% for 3 seconds (dust in legs)
  Phase 5 (reset):   Crust reforms after 20 seconds

AVOIDANCE:
  Walk around it. Or use shovel to probe ahead (if upgraded).
  Upgraded shovel automatically highlights sinkholes nearby.

GODOT IMPL:
  StaticBody2D (normal ground tile)
  Area2D child (warning trigger, radius 36px)
  Timer (0.8s before damage)
  AnimatedSprite2D (crack frames: safe/warning/broken/reform)

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 02: CHEMICAL PUDDLE
Location: Muynak, near old industrial areas
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Pesticide/fertilizer runoff pooled on the ground.
  Iridescent greenish color — visually distinct.

BEHAVIOR:
  Always visible (no stealth phase)
  Rustam walks into it → 5 damage per second
  Vision gets slightly blurred (shader effect: wiggle)
  Lasts until Rustam exits the puddle Area2D

AVOIDANCE:
  Go around. Or use spray_can to neutralize it
  (interacting with the puddle while holding spray_can
   converts it to safe ground, adds 1 to tree count
   since it enriches soil)

VARIANTS:
  Small (2x2 tiles)  → 5 dmg/sec
  Large (4x4 tiles)  → 8 dmg/sec
  Spreading (grows over time if left) → max 6x6 tiles

GODOT IMPL:
  Area2D with animated sprite (slow shimmer loop)
  _process: deal damage while body_inside array has player
  Shader: mild distortion on camera when inside

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 03: RUSTED WIRE SNARE
Location: Muynak ship graveyard
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Old fishing wire or ship rigging left in the sand.
  Nearly invisible until you walk into it.

BEHAVIOR:
  Phase 1: Thin 1px line across a tile gap (hard to see)
  Phase 2: Player crosses it → snared, cannot move 2 seconds
  Phase 3: Takes 10 damage from the wire cutting in
  Phase 4: Wire breaks, player free

AVOIDANCE:
  Look carefully. Move slowly in ship graveyard.
  If you have rake equipped, it auto-clears wires on walk
  (upgraded rake passive from Push 19)

VISUAL:
  1-2px horizontal or diagonal line across a doorway/gap
  Slight glint animation (1 frame flash every 3 seconds)
  Once broken: wire pieces on ground (decorative, no collision)

GODOT IMPL:
  Area2D (very thin CollisionShape2D, RectangleShape 32x2)
  On body_entered: freeze player (can_move = false), timer 2s
  AnimatedSprite2D: idle glint / snap / broken

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 04: COLLAPSING FLOOR PLANK
Location: Inside abandoned buildings (Hometown ruins)
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Rotted wooden floor inside derelict Soviet-era buildings.
  Rustam can explore these optionally for diary pages.

BEHAVIOR:
  Phase 1: Planks look slightly darker than safe floor
  Phase 2: Rustam stands on it → creaking visual (shake)
            0.5s delay
  Phase 3: Plank breaks → Rustam drops, takes 12 damage
  Phase 4: Hole remains for rest of level session
           (blocks that path — must find another way)

AVOIDANCE:
  Don't step on dark-colored floor tiles.
  Or move quickly across (under 0.3s = no trigger)

GODOT IMPL:
  StaticBody2D with AnimatedSprite2D
  On body_entered: start shake tween + 0.5s timer
  If player still on it after timer: disable collision,
  play break animation, deal damage
  Once broken: replace with hole sprite (no collision)

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 05: DUST DEVIL (MINI TORNADO)
Location: Open desert, Seafloor level
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Small wandering dust tornado.
  Natural hazard, not placed deliberately.
  Moves semi-randomly across the open seabed.

BEHAVIOR:
  Spawns at map edge, wanders across level in 20-30s
  Contact: pushes Rustam 48px in impact direction
            + 8 damage + 1s disoriented (controls inverted)
  Disappears at opposite map edge

WARNING:
  Sound cue 3 seconds before it reaches player area
  Sand particle trail visible from far away

AVOIDANCE:
  Watch the trail, step to the side
  Dust Drifter enemies get BUFFED near Dust Devils
  (they absorb the dust and move 30% faster)

GODOT IMPL:
  CharacterBody2D (not enemy — no state machine)
  Moves in straight line with slight sine wave drift
  Area2D child: on body_entered apply knockback + damage
  AnimatedSprite2D: spinning sand column 4 frames
  Spawner node at map edge on random Timer (15-40s interval)

──────────────────────────────────────────
TRAP 06: SOVIET MINE (Rare — Chapter 4 only)
Location: Border area near the wall
──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT IS:
  Old Soviet-era pressure mine left in the border zone.
  Rare. Only 2-3 placed in the entire Border level.
  The Kazakh guard warns about them in dialogue.

BEHAVIOR:
  Buried — only a tiny metallic glint visible
  Step on it → 2 second countdown visual (red flash)
  If not moved away: 35 damage (nearly fatal)
  If moved away in time: mine detonates, leaves crater tile

AVOIDANCE:
  Guard tells you exactly where they are in dialogue.
  Listen to his warnings. Walk the path he shows you.

GODOT IMPL:
  StaticBody2D (underground, tiny Sprite2D for glint)
  On body_entered: start 2s countdown (AnimatedSprite2D blink)
  Timer: if player still in range → deal 35 damage
  Post-explosion: swap to crater tile sprite

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TRAP PLACEMENT RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. Never place a trap in the ONLY path forward
   (always leave at least one safe route around it)
2. First instance of each trap type must be in a safe
   area where Rustam can observe it without triggering it
3. Cluster similar traps together (chemical puddles near
   industrial areas, sinkholes near dry open seabed)
4. Never place traps within 4 tiles of an NPC
5. After the magic well rebirth, Rustam moves faster —
   reconsider wire snare placement post-well
