GLOBAL RULES
─────────────
Canvas size per sprite: 32x32px (draw at 256x256, export at 32x32)
Export method: nearest-neighbor downscale (use pixel_downscale.py)
Spritesheet grid: 4 frames wide per animation row
Import in Godot: Filter Mode NEAREST, Mipmaps OFF
All humanoid characters face RIGHT by default (flip_h for left)
Outline: 1px dark outline on all characters and enemies
Max colors per character: 14 colors including outline and shadow
