ARAL SEA REVIVAL — REVISED PLOT & ENDING
New Chapter 5: The Time Gate / USSR Policy Arc
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OVERVIEW OF THE CHANGE
───────────────────────
Original ending: Rustam blows the northern wall,
water flows in, he swims into the sunset. Good ending.

New ending: The magic well does not just heal Rustam.
It opens a GATE backward in time. Rustam can physically
travel to 1950s-1960s Soviet Uzbekistan and influence
the political decisions that caused the disaster.
He does not erase history — he plants the seed of change
that eventually, 70 years later, allows the sea to return.
Peaceful. Historical. Earned. Emotionally devastating in
the best possible way.

REVISED CHAPTER STRUCTURE (5 chapters now)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CHAPTER 1 — COMING HOME (unchanged)
  Hometown → meet Bibi → hear about the sea
  Goal: understand what was lost

CHAPTER 2 — THE GRAVEYARD (mostly unchanged)
  Muynak → ships → Kamola → enemies
  Goal: restore the surface, learn the scale of damage

CHAPTER 3 — THE SEAFLOOR (expanded)
  Dry seabed → traps → soil restoration → the well
  NEW: After activating the well and being reborn,
  a second vision appears — not just water, but
  a flickering image of a government office, 1950s.
  Bibi's voice: "The well remembers everything.
  Even the decisions that should not have been made."
  Goal: restore the physical land AND discover the gate

CHAPTER 4 — THE GATE (NEW CHAPTER)
  The well opens a circular portal at its base.
  Rustam must choose: go through, or stay.
  If he stays: original ending (good, bittersweet)
  If he goes through: Chapter 5 begins.

  The portal room:
    Circular chamber at the bottom of the well
    Walls show flickering images — Soviet maps, cotton fields,
    men in suits pointing at rivers on paper
    The gate pulses gold and white
    Bibi's photo (from collectibles) glows in inventory

  Rustam: "If I could go back... not to fight.
           Just to speak to one person.
           Would that be enough?"
  Gate: [It responds to his intention — opens fully]

CHAPTER 5 — TASHKENT, 1953 (NEW)
  Rustam arrives in black-and-white (desaturated palette).
  The world is pixel art but the color is washed out —
  everything in greys, creams, and faded Soviet reds.
  He cannot fight. He has no tools.
  He can only WALK and TALK.

  The level: A government administrative building.
  Soviet-era corridors, maps on walls, typewriters.
  NPCs are Soviet planners in uniform — passive, busy.

  THE GOAL:
  Find Minister Zorin — the fictional official who
  signed the order to divert the Amu Darya river.
  He is about to sign the second, larger diversion order.
  Rustam must convince him not to.

  HOW:
  Rustam cannot attack him. Cannot steal the document.
  He must collect 3 EVIDENCE ITEMS in the building first:
    - A hydrologist's warning report (ignored, in a trash bin)
    - A photograph of the Aral Sea at full size (on a wall)
    - A letter from a Karakalpak fisherman (lost in a drawer)

  Then bring them to Zorin.
  Present them one by one in dialogue.

  ZORIN DIALOGUE ARC:
  Item 1 (report):
    Zorin: "That is one scientist's opinion."
    Rustam: "It is a prediction. And it came true."
  Item 2 (photograph):
    Zorin: "The sea is large. It can spare the water."
    Rustam: "Not forever. Look at this photograph.
              In seventy years — nothing but dust."
  Item 3 (letter):
    Zorin: "...This fisherman. He has children?"
    Rustam: "His grandchildren cannot breathe clean air."
    [Long silence. Zorin looks at the map. Looks at Rustam.]
    Zorin: "How do you know all this?"
    Rustam: "I lived it."

  OUTCOME:
  Zorin does not cancel the project entirely.
  (Rustam cannot change everything — that would be false)
  But he adds a preservation clause to the order:
  Minimum water flow must be maintained to the sea.
  A tiny change. A seed.

  Zorin: "I will add a footnote. That is all I can do."
  Rustam: "A footnote saved the North Aral Sea in 2005.
           A footnote is enough."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FINAL ENDING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Rustam steps back through the gate.

The well chamber begins to fill with water — slowly,
from the bottom, clean and cold.

He swims upward through the well shaft.

He surfaces in the restored Aral Sea.

DIFFERENCE FROM ORIGINAL ENDING:
This time the water is not just from the broken wall.
It comes from EVERYWHERE — north AND south.
Because the preservation clause Zorin signed meant
slightly more water reached the sea every decade.
It was not enough to stop the disaster.
But it was enough to make the restoration possible
when Rustam finally came home.

The color returns to the world as he surfaces.
Greens and blues flood the palette.

Bibi is standing on the new shore.
She is young in this moment — the photograph version of her.
She waves.

Rustam: "Was it real?"
[No answer. Just water. Just light. Just the sea.]

Final image: the satellite view, 1984 → 2026.
The sea, partially restored.
Text: "The North Aral Sea began recovering in 2005
       after the construction of the Kokaral Dam.
       The South Aral Sea remains dry.
       The work continues."

CHAPTER 5 MECHANICS (minimal — story-focused)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MOVEMENT:   Normal walking only. No running.
            (The past feels heavy. Slow movement is intentional.)

PALETTE:    Full desaturation. Only Rustam has color —
            his jacket stays dark navy. Everything else grey.

ENEMIES:    None. Zero combat in Chapter 5.
            The tension is entirely emotional.

TRAPS:      None. No danger.
            The danger already happened — 70 years ago.

TOOLS:      None. Inventory is hidden in this chapter.

MUSIC:      Single piano track. No percussion.
            Melancholic but not hopeless.

TIME LIMIT: Soft limit — a clock on the wall of the
            government building ticks. If Rustam takes
            too long, the signing ceremony begins and
            Zorin becomes unavailable (bad ending branch).
            Player has ~8 real minutes to collect all 3 items.

BAD ENDING BRANCH:
  If Zorin signs before Rustam presents evidence:
  Rustam returns to present. The sea is restored only
  partially (original ending visual). Still hopeful.
  But the text reads: "Some changes come too late to prevent.
  But never too late to begin healing."
