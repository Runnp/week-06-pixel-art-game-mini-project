WHAT THIS PUSH BUILDS
──────────────────────
The Seafloor is what used to be underwater.
Every tile you walk on was once the bottom of the Aral Sea.
This is the most open, most desolate, most important level.
It is also where the game's emotional core lives — the well.

The visual language shifts here:
  - Muynak was orange-brown (rust and sand)
  - The Seafloor is WHITE and GREY (salt, bone, emptiness)
  - Only Rustam's planted trees bring color back in

MAP SIZE: 48 tiles wide x 32 tiles tall = 1536 x 1024 pixels
(Biggest level. Needs Camera2D limits set to map bounds.)

CAMERA LIMITS (set in Player.tscn Camera2D):
  Limit Left:   0
  Limit Top:    0
  Limit Right:  1536
  Limit Bottom: 1024


ZONE LAYOUT
────────────

ZONE A — Entry from Muynak (top 6 rows)
  Salt flat transition — coming from the north
  First sinkhole trap visible but avoidable (tutorial trap)
  Soil patch x3 (spray to treat — triggers tree growth)
  Tree spot x3 (plant saplings after spraying)
  Small rusted anchor half-buried in ground (decorative)

ZONE B — The Open Seabed (middle 18 rows, largest zone)
  Pure white salt crust dominant tile
  Scattered grey cracked earth for variety
  THIS IS WHERE MOST GAMEPLAY HAPPENS:

  Left side:
    Ship skeleton (just bones of a very old wooden vessel)
    Chemical puddle x2 (spread variant, starts small)
    Salt Crust Crawler enemy x2 (burrow trap enemies)
    Soil patch x4 + Tree spot x4

  Center:
    THE MAGIC WELL — tile position (24, 18) — exact center
    Surrounded by a ring of unique "ancient stone" tiles
    8 tile radius around well is clear — open ceremonial space
    Diary page 05 hidden in the stone ring
    Cotton Specter mini-boss patrols the ring perimeter
    (blocks access to well until defeated)

  Right side:
    Rusted Soviet-era measurement buoy (old water level marker)
    Interact: "[This marker once measured water depth.]"
              "[The last recorded depth: 0.0 meters. 1989.]"
    Dust Devil spawner (right edge, spawns every 25s)
    Photo pickup: photo_aral_blue (hidden behind buoy)
    Pesticide Shade enemy x1 (poisons tiles near buoy)

ZONE C — The South Wall (bottom 8 rows)
  Dense salt flat — almost impassable without rake
  This is the "locked" area before the well is activated
  After well activates: path opens here toward the gate
  Time Gate appears at tile (24, 30) — deep south center
  Surrounded by golden shimmer tiles (post-well only, set visible=false until then)

  T_SALT_FLAT     pure white salt, main tile

  T_ANCIENT_STONE darker grey, worn smooth — well surroundings
  T_POISONED      sickly greenish tile (Chemical puddle aftermath)
  T_RESTORED      NEW TILE — appears when Rustam treats soil
                  Green-grey, shows life returning
                  Replace T_CRACKED_GREY after spray_can used


RESTORED TILE MECHANIC
───────────────────────
When Rustam uses spray_can on a soil_patch Area2D:
  1. soil_patch.gd calls GameManager.plant_tree()
  2. The tile beneath that Area2D changes to T_RESTORED
  3. After 10 seconds, a small green sprout sprite appears
  4. After tree_spot.gd is used nearby, full tree sprite grows

This is the most visual payoff in the game.
Walking through a restored section should feel
completely different from the dead salt flats.
Greener. Softer. Like something is breathing again.

Code to add to soil_patch.gd _play_spray_effect():

  # Change the tile beneath us to restored tile
  var tilemap := get_tree().get_first_node_in_group("tilemap")
  if tilemap:
      var cell := tilemap.local_to_map(global_position)
      tilemap.set_cell(cell, 0, Vector2i(0, 2))  # T_RESTORED coords
