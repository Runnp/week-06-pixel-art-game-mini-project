CORE NPC PHILOSOPHY
───────────────────
All NPCs in this game are PASSIVE. They never attack Rustam.
They never block his path. They never demand anything.
They exist to:
  1. Give context and emotional weight to the world
  2. Offer optional dialogue that enriches the story
  3. Provide tools, hints, or items when approached
  4. React to the world changing as Rustam restores it

Think of them like real people in a quiet, grieving town —
not quest-givers in a fantasy RPG. They have their own
routines. They are tired. Some are hopeful. Some have given up.

──────────────────────────────────────────
NPC 01: GRANDMOTHER BIBI
Chapter: 1 (Hometown)
Role: Emotional anchor, memory keeper
──────────────────────────────────────────
PERSONALITY:
  Warm but deeply sad. Speaks slowly.
  Uses Uzbek proverbs naturally in conversation.
  Does not complain — just remembers.
  Never asks Rustam to do anything directly.
  She trusts that he already knows what must be done.

ROUTINE (passive behavior loop):
  Morning → sits on her doorstep, looks east
  Midday  → waters a single dying plant in her yard
  Evening → goes inside, lamp light visible in window

MOVEMENT:
  Never leaves her yard. Walks 3-4 tiles max.
  Speed: very slow (30px/s)
  Pauses every 8 seconds to "rest and look at horizon"

DIALOGUE STATES:
  First meeting  → full story of the sea (kamola.json)
  After chapter 1 done → "Go child. Come back with good news."
  After well activated → "I dreamed of water last night."
  After restoration → "I knew you would do it. I always knew."

SPRITE NEEDS:
  idle_sit    2 frames   (sitting, slight breathing)
  idle_stand  2 frames   (standing, looking away)
  walk        3 frames   (very slow shuffle)
  Size: 24x28px (shorter than Rustam — she is elderly)

PASSIVE NPC BEHAVIOR — GODOT IMPLEMENTATION RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All NPCs share these rules in code:

1. NEVER use NavigationAgent2D to chase player
2. Use ONLY pre-defined waypoint paths for movement
3. Always face player when player is within 3 tiles
4. Play idle animation between waypoints
5. Freeze routine during active dialogue
6. Resume routine 2 seconds after dialogue ends
7. Never overlap with player collision
8. process_mode = PAUSABLE (pause during cutscenes)

NPC BASE SCRIPT STRUCTURE:
  state: IDLE / WALKING / TALKING
  waypoints: Array[Vector2]  (set in Inspector)
  current_waypoint: int
  face_player_range: float = 96.0  (3 tiles)
  routine_timer: float

FACING LOGIC (runs every frame, overrides movement):
  if player within face_player_range:
    rotate sprite toward player
    (just flip_h for left/right,
     swap animation for up/down)
